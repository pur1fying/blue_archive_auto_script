Source snapshot: equip_stages_cn.json (version 3), derived from
https://schaledb.brightsu.cn/stage/ . Only calculation fields are retained;
duplicated labels, UI code, images, pretty copies and legacy files are omitted.
The snapshot covers CN missions 1–30 and equipment T1–T10. It is not represented
as current JP/Global data. No network request occurs during calculation.

Weighted greedy selection and rank-up costs follow the reference algorithms,
with explicit budgets, strict validation, actual expected-yield accounting,
and no automatic edits to the host's sweep configuration.
